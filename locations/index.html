What should I check locally?

Before roofing work begins, identify who handles the property and the proposed scope. That can involve a building department, a separate historic-review process, or association requirements supplied by the owner.

A ZIP code helps locate a request, but it does not establish a permit answer. Ask the contractor to confirm jurisdiction and approval responsibilities for the actual address.

Statewide resources

Georgia publishes its current construction codes and amendments. Use them alongside the local authority's instructions for the actual work.

For regional weather background, see the NWS north and central Georgia severe-storm guidance. Regional weather history cannot establish damage at an individual property.

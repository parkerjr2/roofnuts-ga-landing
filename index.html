Questions people ask before they request

What is RoofNuts?

A referral service. We connect homeowners with independent roofing contractors in Georgia. Participating contractors pay us for referrals. We don't do the work ourselves.

Is the inspection free?

Yes. [Keep this answer only if every participating contractor offers a free inspection. Otherwise: "Most participating contractors offer a free inspection; the contractor confirms this when they reach out."]

How many companies will contact me?

One. Your request goes to a single participating contractor for your ZIP. It is not shared with a list.

Do I have to be home?

Usually for the walkthrough at the end, so the contractor can show you what they found. Confirm the details when they reach out.

What if nothing is wrong?

Then that should be the answer, and you'll know when to check again. You are never obligated to buy anything.

Can I use the findings for insurance?

Any photos or written findings the contractor gives you are yours. Whether a claim makes sense is between you and your insurer.

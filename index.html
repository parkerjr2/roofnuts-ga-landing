Questions

Is the inspection free?

Yes. Every inspection requested through RoofNuts is free, with no obligation. Any repair or replacement work you later choose is agreed directly with the contractor.

How soon will someone contact me?

A participating contractor contacts you within a couple of hours of your request. Inspections are usually done within 24 to 48 hours.

How many companies will contact me?

One. Your request goes to a single participating contractor for your ZIP.

What should I mention in my request?

Anything you've noticed helps the contractor prepare:

  • A brown ring or damp spot on a ceiling.
  • Dents on gutters, vents, or the mailbox after a storm, and the storm date.
  • Shingle grit collecting at the downspouts.
  • Lifted, curled, or missing shingles, and which side of the house.
  • Roof age, if you know it. "Unknown" is fine.
Do I have to be home?

Usually for the walkthrough at the end, so the contractor can show you what they found. Confirm the details with them.

What if nothing is wrong?

Then that should be the answer. You are never obligated to buy anything.

Can I use the findings for insurance?

Any photos or written findings the contractor gives you are yours. Whether a claim makes sense is between you and your insurer.

Which parts of Georgia are covered?

Availability depends on the ZIP. The form checks coverage before asking for contact details, and the locations list below shows current areas.

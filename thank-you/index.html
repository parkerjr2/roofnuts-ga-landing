Your RoofNuts Request Was Received

Thank you for explaining what you need. Your request has been submitted for a location and project-fit check. This is not an appointment confirmation.

What happens next?

The inspection is free. A participating contractor will typically contact you within a couple of hours, and inspections are usually completed within 24 to 48 hours. Before agreeing to a visit, confirm what it includes; any repair or replacement work beyond the inspection is separate and would need your authorization.

If you need to correct the property or contact details, contact RoofNuts and refer to the request you just submitted.

Prepare for the conversation

Keep any installation or repair records you already have. Note when the concern began and where you have noticed it. You do not need to climb onto the roof or collect new roof-level photographs.

While you wait, you can review what to ask for in an inspection or how to compare estimates.
